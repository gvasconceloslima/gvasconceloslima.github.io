Research Development

Research Support & Capacity-BuildingCoordinator, Law Research and Innovation Unit (NIP-CCJ) · University of Fortaleza · 2022–February 2025Building practical connections between central research administration, faculty and undergraduate researchers.

The challenge was to bring the university’s research-support initiatives closer to faculty and students in their everyday work. Faculty perceived a distance between central research administration and their academic units, creating a need for more accessible guidance on funding opportunities, institutional programs and research development.

The Research and Innovation Units (NIPs) were established to bridge this gap. They connected central research administration with faculty and students, translating opportunities and institutional procedures into practical support while bringing researchers’ questions and needs into university-level discussions.

As part of the founding team, I helped develop the regulations, calls and operating routines for this new structure. As coordinator of the Law unit, I advised faculty on research and innovation submissions, supported the creation of research groups in the CNPq Research Groups Directory, and helped researchers establish Google Scholar profiles. I combined individual assistance with reusable guidance, turning recurring questions into resources that faculty could consult independently.

I also helped design and deliver an annual training pathway for undergraduate researchers, responding to supervisors’ concerns about students entering projects without foundational research skills. The program covered research design, literature review, scientific writing, presentations and academic publishing. I taught the scientific-presentation module and later developed its recorded lessons and materials.

During this period, the team delivered three training cycles and began transitioning the pathway toward asynchronous learning to accommodate students’ classes and internship commitments. By the time I left, the first stage had been converted to online delivery.

These contributions helped establish a research-support structure within the academic unit, produced practical resources for faculty and created a recurring training pathway for students.
